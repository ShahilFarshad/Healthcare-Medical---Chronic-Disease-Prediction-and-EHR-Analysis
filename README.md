<h2>📖 Project Overview</h2>

<p>
Healthcare organizations generate vast amounts of Electronic Health Record (EHR) data, making it challenging to identify patients at risk of chronic diseases and stroke. This project leverages healthcare analytics, machine learning, and business intelligence techniques to transform raw healthcare data into meaningful insights.
</p>

<p>
The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), feature engineering, machine learning model development, and Power BI dashboard visualization. The objective is to identify disease patterns, understand risk factors, and support data-driven healthcare decision-making.
</p>

<h2>🔍 Key Insights</h2>

<h3>1️⃣ Age as a Major Risk Factor</h3>

<ul>
<li>Stroke cases increase significantly among patients aged 61–80 years.</li>
<li>Patients below 40 years show relatively lower stroke occurrence.</li>
</ul>

<h3>2️⃣ Hypertension and Stroke</h3>

<ul>
<li>Patients diagnosed with hypertension have a higher probability of stroke.</li>
<li>Hypertension remains one of the strongest predictors of stroke risk.</li>
</ul>

<h3>3️⃣ Heart Disease Impact</h3>

<ul>
<li>Patients with heart disease show increased stroke prevalence.</li>
<li>Cardiovascular health plays a significant role in stroke occurrence.</li>
</ul>

<h3>4️⃣ Smoking Status Analysis</h3>

<ul>
<li>Former smokers and current smokers contribute substantially to stroke cases.</li>
<li>Lifestyle habits significantly influence health outcomes.</li>
</ul>

<h3>5️⃣ BMI and Glucose Level Relationship</h3>

<ul>
<li>Higher BMI and elevated glucose levels are associated with increased stroke risk.</li>
<li>Scatter plot analysis reveals clustering among high-risk patients.</li>
</ul>

<h3>6️⃣ Residence Type Distribution</h3>

<ul>
<li>Urban residents account for slightly more stroke cases compared to rural residents.</li>
</ul>
